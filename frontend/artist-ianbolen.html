How Ian Bolen makes it work.

TikTok is Ian's main way of getting his music out. He posts short clips of his songs with a hook on screen that tells you who it's for, like "If you like Steve Lacy, Malcolm Todd, Beabadoobee, Dominic Fike, then let me introduce myself." He's open about the grind too: "posting my music until it finds its audience," and a song with over 300k streams that he says he produced in his room on a 10 year old laptop.

Good music. Ian makes very good music that is good.

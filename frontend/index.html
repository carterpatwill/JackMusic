My Criteria

01

It starts with the music

The music has to catch my ear, a unique voice, a great lyricist, and high quality production, these are all things I look for when investing in an artist.

02

Independent artists only

Quigley Discovery only invests in independent artists and offers an independence guarantee that none of the featured artists are signed to record deals.

03

A world, beyond the song

The artists I invest in are building something bigger than one song: a visual identity, a consistent release cadence, a world people want to follow, not just a track they stream once.

04

What am I willing to invest?

What am I willing to invest in this artist? A playlist add? A follow? Sharing with a friend? Purchasing merch? Buying a concert ticket?

About Quigley Discovery

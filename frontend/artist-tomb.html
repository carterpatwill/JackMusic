More on Tomb

Tomb is about as under-the-radar as it gets — a couple of tracks (DYSTOPIA chief among them) have pulled huge streaming numbers while his actual social footprint stays tiny. No label, no major-adjacent distributor: everything is self-released through his own imprint, KATAKOMB.

What I did find is a real trail of other people's TikToks using his tracks as mood/aesthetic audio across many separate accounts over several months, plus an indie-discovery blog feature — the kind of organic, third-party reuse that's a positive signal in my experience, not a red flag. He simply doesn't have an official YouTube channel yet, which tracks with how early and unfound he still is.

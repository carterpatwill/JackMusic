More on Themba!

Hailing from the Midwest, Indiana native Themba! meshes modern rap sounds with 2000s instrumentals and energy. I hear Ying Yang Twins, Petey Pablo and Lil Jon influences in the instrumentals of Themba!, the self-proclaimed “Crunkstar.”

How Themba! makes it work.

His first major hit came in early 2025 with Calling My Phone, racking up well over a million streams across platforms. He followed it up with Crunkstar, The Answer and Favorite Song with recurring collaborator Rehma. Every time Themba! hops on one of these beats, it's instantly a hit.
